Measurement Sections

This dashboard will be organised by measurement objective. Negative observations are the default basis for graphs and KPIs unless another observation type is selected.
01
Vetting Inspection Observations
Negative by default. Optional Largely as expected and Positive. Trends by vessel, month, quarter and year.
02
Vessel Audit Findings
Audits have findings only. Split by Marine Superintendent, Master and External Auditor. Includes NIL audit count.
03
PGNO Analytics
For negative Vetting Inspection observations. Trends, recurrence and benchmarking by vessel/question.
04
M-SCAT Analytics
Immediate Causes, Basic Causes and Control Areas. Uses AI and manually assigned M-SCAT until manual review is completed.
05
Sector Analytics
Navigation, Cargo and Mooring comparisons: Vetting vs Superintendent Audits vs Master Audits vs External Audits.
